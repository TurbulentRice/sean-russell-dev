## Before

A legacy message-based ingest pipeline. Messages were heavy and carried the data itself, passing
through many layers of processing, validation and mapping. It was slow and opaque, and a lost
message was unrecoverable. Fixing data meant asking the source owners to push again.

## After

- Messages are lightweight **signals**, coalesced by their consumers for true FIFO behaviour
- Fan-out to atomic consumers keeps every message a signal
- Data is fetched **directly from the source**, not carried in the message

## Results

- Messages went from heavy and costly to light and cheap, and process faster
- Support staff can trigger updates on our side instead of waiting on source owners
- More visibility into failures, and more agency over, and confidence in, the data behind the site
